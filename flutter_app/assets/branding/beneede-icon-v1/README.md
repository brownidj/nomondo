# BeneEde icon concept v1

Created with the built-in image generation tool on 10 November 2026. A cream
plate/globe on deep teal, with turquoise and green land shapes, an integrated
fork, a coral smile and golden accent. Broad shapes and no lettering support
recognition at small sizes. The original generated PNG is preserved; 16, 24, 32,
48, 64, 128, 256, 512 and 1024-pixel PNGs are mechanical resamplings with macOS sips.
This concept has been adopted. The canonical app source is ../../beneede-icon.png;
macOS and web icon sets are generated copies. The installed app picks them up
when rebuilt/restarted.

Prompt: One polished square app icon for BeneEde, a worldwide restaurant discovery
and personal dining catalogue. A cream dinner plate that also reads as a globe,
two or three oversized turquoise/leaf-green continent shapes, a warm coral-orange
happy-bite curve, and a bold rounded fork integrated into the plate's left edge.
Deep teal full-bleed background. Flat vector-like geometric style, broad shapes,
clean edges, about 12 percent inset, no text, fine grid, tiny food or flags,
watermark, mockup, photorealism, drop shadow or glossy 3D. Optimized for small sizes.

The complete generation prompt is preserved in prompt.txt. No app launches,
builds or tests were performed. Review the PNGs at actual size before adoption.
